Career Achievement · 2023
  • Nolan refuses to use CGI for effects that can be practically achieved on set — he built a full rotating hallway for Inception.
  • He is one of the few directors to have final cut privilege in Hollywood before winning an Academy Award.
  • Nolan prefers shooting on 70mm IMAX film, often converting theaters for dedicated screenings.
  • He studied English Literature at Cambridge, not film — he learned filmmaking by borrowing the university's 16mm camera on weekends.
  • The script for Memento was inspired by a short story written by his brother Jonathan Nolan.
  • Nolan does not use a mobile phone and has never sent an email.
